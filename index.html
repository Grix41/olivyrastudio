Good to know

Questions before you order

Digital files, shirt fit and delivery — the short version. Full details live on each Etsy listing.

Are these physical products or digital downloads?

Both exist in the shop. The Christmas designs are digital downloads delivered instantly through Etsy. The bookish tees are physical garments printed to order and fulfilled through Etsy.

What files do I get with a digital design?

Each design includes two files of the same artwork: one PNG at 5000 × 5000 px, 300 DPI with a transparent background, and one SVG in scalable vector format. Both are intended for compatible design, cutting and print software.

How do I download my files after buying?

Open your Etsy account, go to Purchases and Reviews, find the order and choose Download Files. Digital downloads are accessed through a web browser rather than the Etsy mobile app.

How do the T-shirts fit and wash?

The tees are unisex Comfort Colors 1717 with a relaxed fit — choose your usual size, or size up for an oversized look. Check the size chart in each listing. Machine wash cold, inside out, with mild detergent; do not bleach; tumble dry low or hang dry; do not iron directly over the print.

Where do you ship, and what about returns?

Apparel is printed to order and fulfilled through Etsy. Delivery estimates, shipping costs and return eligibility are shown on each Etsy listing and at checkout, so you can review them before ordering.

Is the artwork AI-assisted?

Some designs are created with the assistance of AI tools, then reviewed and prepared for print by the studio. This is stated on the relevant Etsy listings as well.
